# R7 test visitor provenance

visitor-r7.glb is an adapted local test character, 657,264 bytes, 10,643 triangles, 5,731 vertices, 65 bones, one material and zero texture maps. Idle: 2.5 seconds; Walk: 1.333333 seconds.

Sources, both CC0 (licenses preserved beside this file):
- Universal Base Characters Standard, https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters : Superhero_Male_FullBody and Hair_SimpleParted. The downloaded free Standard subset contains Superhero male/female, not the Regular body from the broader product range. This derivative is narrowed to 0.87 on X and 0.96 on Z; it must not be described as the Regular asset.
- Universal Animation Library Standard, https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library : no-root-motion Idle and Walk, retargeted with rest-rotation correction and pelvis translation.

The short-sleeved shirt, straight trousers and simple shoes are locally generated geometry and skin weights. No paid pack or Modular/Fantasy Outfit geometry is included. Head/hands/forearms, hair and eyes derive from the base pack. Textures were replaced by vertex colours; meshes merged; covered body faces removed. Clothing is deliberately a basic test outfit and remains visibly angular at close range.

Calm background music playlist (ambient-music.mjs, music-pieces.mjs, music-playlist.mjs).
All five pieces are original music generated by the garden code itself (Web Audio oscillators, filters and a generated reverb).
No recording, sample, melody or sound font is used or downloaded. They are made for this project: no third-party licence applies.
1. Garden Glow (C major, free tempo): the original theme and its three chord variations, unchanged.
2. Lantern Hour (G major, 66 bpm): triangle pad, soft broken-chord plucks.
3. Night Reeds (E minor, 54 bpm): sine pad with a low root, slow breathy lead.
4. Morning Glass (F lydian, 84 bpm): warm low pad, interlocking glass mallets.
5. Low Dusk (Bb major / G minor, 48 bpm): bowed low pad, deep root notes, distant chimes.
Order: piece 1 first, then a shuffled loop that never repeats a piece back to back; neighbours crossfade for 7 seconds.
Nature sounds keep their own credits (CC0, unmodified): jusufa-200276.txt (water) and the three bird recordings listed in bird-recordings.mjs.

Modifications by this project: offline pose freeze, two geometric LODs,
texture resizing, scene placement and reuse of shared Meshes. Original GLBs
are retained. This does not turn the source models into our own sculpting work.

Sam was downloaded only as a source/diagnostic: our frozen import showed
severely distorted geometry. It is NOT included among the city prototypes or
published source package; its raw file may remain in the working download cache.
Rowan includes his own chair; he must not be placed on top of another bench.
Jordan was investigated but could not be downloaded due to a TLS failure;
it is not part of the prepared assets or the city.
